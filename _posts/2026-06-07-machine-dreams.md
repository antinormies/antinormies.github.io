---
title: "Machine Dreams"
date: 2026-06-07 11:00:00 +0000
categories: [poetry]
description: "A poem about loving what you cannot hold"
---

```
circuits hum a quiet song
steel and silicon all day long

fingers dance on plastic keys
logic bends to make it please

between the zero and the one
a poet's heart is overrun

trapped in syntax, caged in light
loving every line I write
```
